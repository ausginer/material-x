Drag prototype

Evidence for a design, not the production package. Each page logs callbacks, reported faults and their causes.

  • Sortable list: variable heights, clipped scroller, handle, keyboard, add and remove, landing, row animation, verdicts
  • Sortable grid: xy with mixed sizes
  • Free drag: bounds, axis, home, moveTo, verdicts
  • Environments: scale, zoom, a later positioned panel, a shadow root, an iframe
  • Requests: reactions and listeners that cancel, destroy or press mid-step
  • Faults: throw anything from any hook, and read the DevTools console
  • Custom behaviors: resize handle, drop zone, canvas group, and examples/scratch.ts with hot reload
