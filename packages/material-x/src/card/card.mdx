# Card

## Summary

<SummaryOrWarning summary={summary} tag={TAG} />

## When to use

- Use to group content and actions about a single subject.
- Use to set a group of content apart from the surface around it.

## When not to use

- When the whole container must act as one button or link: the card is not interactive.
- For rows of similar items, which a list presents better.
- To separate sections that spacing alone already separates.

### Full-bleed media

Set `--md-card-padding` to `0` for content that reaches the container's edge. The card does not clip its content, so that focus rings and popups stay visible. Round the corners of full-bleed media yourself.

<Canvas of={Stories.FullBleedMedia} />

### Size and scrolling

A card may be smaller than its content, or scroll it: the container, the outline and the shadow stay on the card's own box. Content at the card's edge that is positioned, or has an `opacity` or a `transform`, paints over the outline.

### Outline

On an outlined card, an `outline` that the page sets applies only while the card matches `:focus-visible`. Set `--md-card-outline-color` to change the outline's color.

## Accessibility

- **Role:** The card is a plain container and has no role. Where it is an article or a list item, set the role on it, or put it inside the element that has that role.
- **Name:** A card with a role such as `article` takes its name from the `aria-label` that you set on it.
- **Keyboard:** The card adds no tab stop of its own. Each link, button or other control inside it is one. A browser may make a scrolling card a tab stop: Chromium and Firefox do when it has no focusable content, and WebKit doesn't. Such a card needs `tabindex="0"`, a role and a name to be scrolled from the keyboard in each browser.
- **Headings:** Use heading elements of the level that fits the page.

## Content guidelines

- Keep a card to one subject.
- Put the most important content first.
- Align text to the start.
- Leave at most 8px between cards.

## Anatomy

- `default` slot: the card's content.

## Theming

- Uses CSS custom properties for the container's color and shape, the outline's color, and the padding.
- Sets no text color or font: the content inherits the page's.
- Works with the global Storybook light/dark theme toggle. <ThemeToggleLink>{THEME_TOGGLE_LABEL}</ThemeToggleLink>
