## Position

- The bar is `position: sticky` at the block start, with `z-index: 1`. A page rule on `mx-top-app-bar` overrides both, and `position: static` unpins it.
- It pins within its parent, so make it a child of `body`. A `<header>` around it needs `display: contents`, and `height: 100%` on `body` lets the bar scroll away with it.
- It follows the document's scroll only. In another scroll container it is sticky there and stays at rest. `overflow-x: hidden` on both `html` and `body` makes `body` one, so the bar scrolls away. `overflow-x: clip` on `body` doesn't.

## Scrolled state

The bar matches `:state(scrolled)` while the document is scrolled away from its start, and its container changes color. The state is the platform's, as `:hover` is: nothing sets it and no event reports it.

<Source code={stateSnippet} language="css" />

## Accessibility

- **Landmark:** the bar has no role of its own. Set `role="banner"` on the page's own bar.
- **Heading:** slot the heading, at the level the page needs. It takes the bar's text style, unless the page styles it.
- **Focused and targeted content:** a pinned bar covers it unless the page's scroller has `scroll-padding-block-start`. No component can set it for the page.

<Source code={scrollPaddingSnippet} language="css" />

- **Focus:** the bar takes no focus of its own. Tab goes through `lead`, the headline and `trail`, in the document's order, so write them in that order.

## Content guidelines

- The headline isn't truncated. A long one wraps, and the bar grows, so keep it short.

## Anatomy

- `lead` slot: the navigation icon.
- `default` slot: the headline.
- `trail` slot: the actions.

## Material 3 reference and deltas

- Material 3 overview: [https://m3.material.io/components/app-bars/overview](https://m3.material.io/components/app-bars/overview)
- Deltas:
  - Only the small app bar, without a subtitle, a centered headline or search.
  - The scrolled bar changes color and draws no shadow. Content in `surface-container` at the top of a scrolled page shows no edge under it.
  - `mx-icon-button` is 40px wide, with no 48px target, so its icon is 4px nearer the edge.
  - `mx-icon-button` keeps its own icon color in `lead`.
