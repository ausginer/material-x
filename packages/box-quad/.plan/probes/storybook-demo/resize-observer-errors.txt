## The BQ-18 reading after the fix: node packages/box-quad/.plan/probes/storybook-demo/stories-reading.ts

Its output equals stories-reading.txt line for line, apart from that file's three header lines. Each of three runs rewrote two or three of the four dark screenshots, a different set each time, Pointer Delta's included. Every pixel of the two compared (nested-spaces-dark, pointer-delta-dark) is within 1 of the committed screenshot in every channel. Pointer Delta's first render doesn't depend on the change, so the dark captures vary by themselves, and the committed screenshots are kept. All four light screenshots were byte-equal in every run.
