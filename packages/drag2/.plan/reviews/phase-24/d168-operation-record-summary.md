## What was not decided here — routed

- **F-275 is an architecture/contract question, not a remediation one, and is routed to the architect rather than resolved here.** Two ways to close it exist and they are not equivalent: (a) strengthen the test to actually reenter after step 5 (immediately before `scrub`), which would make it fail exactly where D-168 §3's stated property requires, or (b) restate required property 1 at the boundary the shipped code and test actually exhibit — step 4/5, "after `spec.retire` and after `lifetimes.dispose`" — and correct the test's inline comment to match. Choosing between "the property is stricter than what's tested" and "the property was stated stricter than what's true" is a decision about D-168's own text, which this consolidation has no authority to make. Route: architect, bounded to this one question — not a reason to reopen D-168 as a whole.
- **F-276's stronger fix (moving the `activation` assignment past the capture-failure block so the docblock's claim becomes true) is also the architect's call**, per the reviewer pass's own framing — it changes which object owns the lift session between `presentation.use` and the throw. The **minimal fix — correcting the docblock to state the invariant that actually holds ("the record is complete-or-absent; a failed activation after assignment retains it")** — is directly actionable and does not require that trade-off. Route: implementer remediation for the docblock correction; the assignment-relocation alternative is available to the architect if F-275's routing prompts a broader look at this file at the same time.
- **F-277 carries no routing** — recorded for the record per the cleanup pass's own brief, not prescribed, not blocking closure.
