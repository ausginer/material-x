   - strictDepBuilds fails the install on an unreviewed dependency script (p4).

     package.json's `aube` field. allowBuilds was read from package.json's
     `aube.allowBuilds` (p9, p10).

   Reading: per-package exclusions exist. In .npmrc they are one comma-separated
   value; an entry is a name, a `*` pattern, or a name with an exact version or
   an exact union. A pattern can't carry a version. An exclusion covers that
   package only, not its dependencies.
