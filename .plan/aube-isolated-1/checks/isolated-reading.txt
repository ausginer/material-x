Two isolated modes exist. aube's default outside CI is the global virtual store (GVS): node_modules
holds symlinks into <cacheDir>/virtual-store/v1/. Under CI, aube disables the GVS and materializes
the virtual store inside the project, at node_modules/.aube/ ("project-local" below).
`aube install --help`: "--enable-global-virtual-store ... Overrides CI's default per-project
materialization".
