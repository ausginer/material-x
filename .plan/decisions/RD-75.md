#### RD-75 — `@ydinjs/core` holds universal utilities for any design system, material-x's `src/core` what means nothing without material-x, and a workaround that expires stays with its users

**Decided 2026-11-27 by the architect, on `repo/core-placement-rule-1`,** on the owner's acceptance of step (c)'s [plan](https://github.com/ausginer/material-x/blob/21025aa80/.plan/core-placement-1/core-placement-plan.md) at his checkpoint of the same day, relayed by the coordinator, with [Q1](https://github.com/ausginer/material-x/blob/21025aa80/.plan/core-placement-1/core-placement-plan.md#q1--the-workarounds-that-expire) (a). The plan proposed it as `CP-D2`. `RD-71` is the highest decision on any branch, the coordinator reserves `RD-72` for `material-x/select-1`, and step (c)'s other two decisions are `RD-73` and `RD-74`, on `repo/core-placement-1`. So this is the next free number. **It holds without code:** the rule is in [`architecture.md` §Core runtime](../../.agents/docs/architecture.md#core-runtime-ydinjscore) and §Extension points, in this pull request.

**The owner's words,** translated by the coordinator from Mx Architect Explainer's notes, 2026-11-26:

- **On what core is for:** _"I put into core not only what has many users. It is rather the place for universal utilities that anyone who wants to build their own design system can use. That differs from material-x/core, which holds shared utilities that are meaningless without material-x itself."_
- **On the workarounds that expire:** _"By rights they should go too, but I don't want to, precisely because they have an expiry date."_ At his checkpoint on 2026-11-27 he answered Q1 with (a).

**Properties:**

1. **`@ydinjs/core` holds universal utilities, which anyone building a design system can use,** whatever the number of their users in this repository.
2. **material-x's `src/core` holds code that material-x's components share and that means nothing without material-x,** such as its styles, its ripple and its token-bound helpers.
3. **_"No second user"_ is a reason only against `src/core`,** as `RF-118` property 5 uses it for `useSortable`. That property's reason against `@ydinjs/core`, a dependency on drag (`RD-31`), stands.
4. **A workaround with a deletion condition stays where its users are until the condition holds,** and is deleted with it in one unit. Today these are material-x's `FocusForwarding` and `useFocusForwarding` (`src/core/traits/focus-forwarding.ts`), `useHasSlottedPolyfill` (`src/core/polyfills/has-slotted.ts`) and `useAriaNotifyPolyfill` (`src/core/polyfills/aria-notify/`). The plan's unit 3, which would have moved them, isn't needed.
5. **Where it is written:** [`architecture.md`](../../.agents/docs/architecture.md), in _Core runtime_, which states properties 1, 2 and 4 and the reason of property 3, and in _Extension points_' _Core capability_, which points to it. The document has no change record, so what it said before is here.

**Why property 4.** In material-x the three are internal, because `src/core` isn't a public entry of material-x (`files.json`), so each is deleted with its condition and no consumer sees it. In core each would be a public export. Once core is published, deleting one at its expiry breaks a consumer who imported it, and `CONTRIBUTING.md` §4's permanence applies: _"an exported value is a promise you cannot withdraw"_. A public core would carry each until a major release, past its condition. Two of them aren't universal today in any case: `useHasSlottedPolyfill` works only with the build's `hasSlottedFallbackVisitor`, in the private `@ydinjs/vite-custom-element-assets`, and `useAriaNotifyPolyfill` imports a CSS sheet and an HTML template, which core's build doesn't transform.

**Why `architecture.md`.** It already says what each package is for, and its _Extension points_ is where an author decides where new code goes. `CONTRIBUTING.md` is about how source is written, not where.

**What `architecture.md` said before,** at `ceb2c12a3`: _Extension points_' _Core capability_ was _"add an `@ydinjs/core` controller or trait and export it through that package's `files.json`."_ _Core runtime_ said nothing of what belongs in core, and the _Overview_ said, as it still does, that core _"supplies the runtime primitives"_.

**Not adopted:**

- **Q1 (b), all three workarounds move to core.** That meets _"by rights they should go too"_. It needs a transform for CSS and HTML in core's build, `hasSlottedFallbackVisitor` published or core documented as needing that private package, and a breaking release at each expiry.
- **Q1 (c), `FocusForwarding` moves and the two polyfills stay.** It has no asset and no build dependency, so it is the only one that is universal today. It still expires with Firefox (`repo:RF-33`), and its deletion would still be a breaking change of a published core.
- **The rule in `CONTRIBUTING.md`.** Its parts are about how source is written and how much it costs, and it names no package's purpose.
