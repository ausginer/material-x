# The radio's keyboard-focused fixture after Tab, Chromium 158, local, slot b's temporary worktree at aca446b94 (origin/main),
# 2026-11-27. The fixture and steps are radio.visual.browser.test.ts's "should match the keyboard-focused radio" on main:
# settle, then Tab, with no settle after it. Run from packages/material-x with
#   aube exec vitest run --project browser tests/<probe>.browser.test.ts
# with the probe placed in tests/ while it ran.
#
# Read:
# - Two transitions run in the radio's shadow root when Tab returns: `color` on the unchecked icon (mx-icon.off, 200 ms,
#   --_check-duration) and `opacity` on the ripple's ::before (15 ms). The fixture's `transition: none` rule matches
#   `.mx-test-fixture *`, which doesn't reach into a shadow root.
# - The icon's computed colour reaches on-surface, rgb(25, 29, 19), about 150 ms after Tab returns.
# - Finishing the shadow root's animations after Tab (settleAnimations) leaves none running, and the colour is final at once.
# - Two settled images: 711b664f when the transitions end by themselves, 3377e0b9 when they are finished. They differ in
#   9 pixels on the focus layer's edge, by 1 or 2 levels. Run 2 read 711b664f twice in 10 at 150 ms after finishing.
# - Against 711b664f, with pixelmatch's threshold of 0.01 and without its antialiasing exemption (an upper bound of what
#   the matcher counts): both settled images and two late transient ones are 0 pixels over; three transient images read
#   with no wait are 38, 55 and 76 over.
# - The local images aren't compared with the committed baseline: they differ from it in 142 and 151 pixels, with colour
#   fringes on the glyph in the baseline. That is the environment, and says nothing here.
